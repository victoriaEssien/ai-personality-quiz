# Project brief

## AI Personality Quiz

A personality quiz where both of us answer a series of questions. The questions are
designed to reveal how we think, make decisions, react to situations, interact with
people, and so on.

At the end, the AI analyses the answers and generates a personalised personality
profile for each person.

Instead of generic results like "you're an introvert", the AI creates a more
interesting description of the kind of person you are: your tendencies, strengths,
how you approach situations, your decision-making style, social behaviour.

Example:

> **Your Personality: The Strategic Free Spirit**
> You like having a plan, but you don't like feeling restricted by it. You tend to
> think things through before making important decisions, but when something
> genuinely excites you, you're willing to throw the plan out the window.

The AI-generated personality analysis is the main feature.

## Later

A "How well do you know your friend?" mode: you answer questions predicting how your
friend would respond, then compare that against their actual answers.
